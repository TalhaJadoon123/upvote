## Why Upvote exists

Reddit is the most-repeated confirmed growth channel for small software products. A
founder told me Reddit drove more growth than SEO, ads and social combined, and closed
a sale in four days with four or five offers on the table.

The problem is not distribution. It is writing.

Developers do not want to be content creators. Not one person has ever closed a tab
because a changelog entry was genuinely interesting. So we do the part nobody wants
to do, and we do it badly on purpose: Upvote does not sound like a brand. It sounds
like you.

<Callout title="The one rule">
  Nothing posts without your approval. Upvote drafts, you decide.
</Callout>

## What it does

1. **Watches your repos.** Releases, merged `shippable` PRs and closed bugs become
   shipping moments. Docs-only changes, dependency bumps and lockfile updates are
   dropped before anything is written.
2. **Learns your voice.** Past posts, comments, tweets, READMEs and commit messages
   become a style profile: 29 measured dimensions plus the phrases and words you
   actually use.
3. **Drafts five options.** Story, show-and-tell, question, data and comment reply —
   each with a subreddit, an hour, a flair and a first comment.
4. **Scores every draft 0-100 against your voice.** Under 85 and it regenerates
   rather than reaching you.
5. **Posts from your Reddit account.** Your token, your karma, your reputation.
6. **Tracks what it produced.** Upvotes, clicks, signups and revenue per post, and
   feeds the winners back into the next batch.
